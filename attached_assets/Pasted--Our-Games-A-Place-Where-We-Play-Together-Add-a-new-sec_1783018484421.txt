🎮 Our Games – A Place Where We Play Together

Add a new section to the website called "Our Games."

This should be a dedicated space where we can spend time together by playing games online, no matter how far apart we are.

Main Idea

When either of us opens the Our Games page, we should see a beautiful collection of multiplayer games designed for two players.

The interface should feel modern, romantic, and premium, matching the overall theme of the website.

---

Multiplayer Experience

Every game should support real-time online multiplayer, allowing both of us to join the same game room and play together from our own devices.

When one person creates a game, the other should be able to join instantly.

Both players should see each other's moves in real time without refreshing the page.

Show:

- Online status
- Invite or Join button
- Player names
- Turn indicator
- Game timer (where applicable)
- Victory and celebration animations

---

Games to Include

Create a beautiful game library that includes as many two-player games as possible, such as:

- ♟️ Chess
- 🎲 Ludo
- 🃏 UNO
- ⚫ Carrom
- ❌ Tic-Tac-Toe
- 🔴 Connect Four
- 🧩 Memory Card Match
- 🧠 Quiz Challenge
- ✍️ Draw & Guess
- 🎯 Dots and Boxes
- 🎴 Playing Cards
- 🪨 Rock, Paper, Scissors
- 🔢 Number Guessing
- 🎮 Mini Arcade Games
- 🧩 Puzzle Challenges

Include additional fun two-player games wherever possible to make the collection feel complete.

---

Beautiful Design

Design the Games page using:

- Glassmorphism cards
- Smooth animations
- Romantic gradients
- Floating hearts
- Premium icons
- Soft glowing effects
- Interactive hover animations

Every game card should have a unique design and feel exciting to open.

---

Game Lobby

Before entering a game, show a beautiful lobby where we can:

- Create a private room
- Join an existing room
- See who is online
- Invite each other instantly
- Start the game together

The lobby should feel elegant and simple to use.

---

After Every Match

At the end of every game:

- Show the winner.
- Display celebration animations.
- Keep track of wins, losses, and draws.
- Offer options to play again or choose another game.

Winning should feel rewarding, while losing should still feel fun and encouraging.

---

Final Goal

This should not feel like an ordinary games page.

It should become our own private gaming space where we can laugh, compete, create memories, and enjoy spending time together even when we are miles apart.

The Games section should make our long-distance relationship feel closer by giving us a fun and meaningful way to connect anytime, anywhere.
