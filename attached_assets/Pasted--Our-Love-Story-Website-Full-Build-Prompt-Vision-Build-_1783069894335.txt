# 💌 Our Love Story Website — Full Build Prompt

## Vision
Build a premium, cinematic, interactive love-story website — not a normal anniversary page. Every section should feel unique, emotional, and beautiful, like turning pages in a living memory book. The end goal: she scrolls through our journey and reaches the last page in happy tears. This should be one of the most unforgettable gifts she's ever received.

Overall aesthetic to apply everywhere: glassmorphism, romantic gradients, floating hearts, soft glow effects, cinematic transitions, elegant scroll-based animations, premium typography, dreamy backgrounds, magical hover states, 60 FPS smoothness, mobile-first and fully responsive. No section should feel repetitive — each one gets its own visual identity.

---

## 1. Landing Screen (before the site opens)
- Full-viewport (100vw x 100vh) intro screen with soft romantic lighting and subtle background animation.
- **PRATHISH** in large premium typography, fixed on the left. **SRADHA** in matching style, fixed on the right.
- A large glowing heart floats/pulses at top-center.
- User drags the heart down to the exact center — it follows finger/cursor smoothly.
- On reaching center: heart snaps into place with glow, sparkles, and soft haptic vibration (supported devices).
- Heart lock triggers: bright glow burst → particle animation spreads across screen → both names fade and drift toward center → premium zoom-and-fade transition into the main site.
- No "Click to Enter" button or text — entry happens only through the drag gesture.

## 2. Hero Section
Headline: **"The moment I found you, my heart already knew its home."**
Large, elegant typography with soft entrance animation and a gently animated romantic background.

## 3. Anniversary Badge
Replace the plain date line with a glowing glass badge:
> ❤️ July 3, 2026
> 365 Days of Love
> "Every day with you has been my favorite chapter."

Soft ambient glow animation, floating slightly, glass-card styling.

## 4. Relationship Timer (on the home page)
An interactive card: **"How Long Have We Been Together?"**
Tapping it smoothly expands into a live counter showing Years / Months / Days / Hours / Minutes / Seconds, updating in real time with an elegant unfold animation.

## 5. Our Story — Interactive Storybook Timeline
Merge all narrative sections ("Our Story," "Story First," "Story of Us") into **one** immersive storybook-style timeline covering the full journey from first meeting to today.
- Chapters open like turning pages of a book.
- Each chapter: photos, soft transitions, floating hearts, cinematic pacing.
- Should feel like reading our story, not scrolling a webpage.

## 6. "The Universe I Found You"
Keep the existing content but redesign every card individually — no two cards should look alike. Draw from: floating planets, starfields, glass cards, heart particle animations, aurora gradients, dreamy lighting. Weave in our existing photos naturally.

## 7. Six Memories Challenge (unlock game)
Photo gallery replaced with a locked-memory game. Each memory unlocks only after answering its question correctly.
- ✅ Correct → success animation → photo reveal → romantic message.
- ❌ Incorrect → "❌ Incorrect Answer — Try again, my love ❤️"

## 8. Proposal Video Reveal
Show a card: **"🎁 Tap to Reveal."** Tapping triggers a smooth cinematic transition that reveals the proposal video — no autoplay before the tap.

## 9. Private Chat Room (on the home page)
Move "Our Private Space" to be a main homepage feature — styled as a premium, exclusive private messaging room for just the two of you.

## 10. Our Games — Play Together Space
A dedicated multiplayer hub, styled like the rest of the site (glassmorphism, gradients, floating hearts, premium icons).

**Real-time multiplayer:** create/join a room, live moves with no refresh, online status, invite/join button, player names, turn indicator, timer where relevant, victory/celebration animations.

**Game library:** Chess, Ludo, UNO, Carrom, Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four, Memory Card Match, Quiz Challenge, Draw & Guess, Dots and Boxes, Playing Cards, Rock Paper Scissors, Number Guessing, Mini Arcade Games, Puzzle Challenges — plus any other fun two-player games that fit.

**Lobby:** create a private room, join a room, see who's online, invite instantly, start together — kept simple and elegant.

**Post-match:** show winner, celebration animation, running win/loss/draw record, play-again or switch-game options. Every game stays inside the site — in-app pages/modals only, no external tabs or redirects.

## 11. Real-Time Voice Chat
Private, low-latency voice calling between just the two registered users.
- Start Voice Chat / End Voice Chat / Mute-Unmute buttons.
- Clear indicators for: connected, muted, other person speaking.
- Fully private — accessible only between the two of you.

## 12. Polish & Fixes
- **Number Guessing Game:** fix number generation, guess validation, "too high/low" hints, win detection, and clean restart — smooth and bug-free.
- **Background music:** remove entirely. No autoplay, no restart on refresh, silent by default — sound only happens intentionally via voice chat.

## Final Goal
Not a website — a journey. A digital love story where every click reveals another memory, every animation feels intentional, and every detail says *you are loved, chosen, and cherished.* Built with maximum creativity, elegance, and emotional storytelling.
